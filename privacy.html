Privacy policy — AHG Backup

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What the app does

AHG Backup uploads the clinic's own backup files from the clinic's own computer to the clinic's own Google Drive account.

Google data it accesses

It asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and manage only the files it creates itself. It cannot read any other file in the Google Drive account.

How data is handled

Files are encrypted on the clinic's computer before they are uploaded. The data is not shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or for training AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's "Third-party apps & services" page. Files the app created can be deleted from Google Drive.

Contact

Questions about this tool can be sent to the clinic's IT administrator through the clinic's usual contact channels.
